The proposal adds mETH to the Compound III USDT market with a borrow collateral factor of 80 percent and a supply cap of 4,000 mETH, and says Gauntlet, the DAO's risk provider, set those numbers. That is the situation clause 2 covers.

The cap bounds how much of the market can rest on one liquid staking token, which also sits within clause 1. Neither action moves treasury funds or touches a timelock, so clauses 3 and 4 do not apply. For.

An implementation upgrade is not a timelock change, so clause 4 is not engaged, but the diff should have been summarised in the proposal itself. For, with lower confidence for that reason.
