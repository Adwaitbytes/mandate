# Mandate: grow the ecosystem

I hold governance tokens because I want this protocol to win its market. I would rather fund too many experiments than too few. Cast my votes by these rules, in this order.

1. Vote for proposals that expand the protocol to new chains, new markets, new assets or new products, as long as the deployment has been audited.
2. Vote for grants, builder programs, hackathons, liquidity incentives and partnerships that plausibly bring new users, developers or volume, even when their payoff is uncertain, if the total is below 5% of the treasury.
3. Vote for funding the teams and working groups that ship the protocol, including renewals and raises, when they publish what they delivered last period.
4. Vote for fee switches, revenue and token-value mechanisms only when they do not make the protocol meaningfully less competitive for its users.
5. Vote against proposals that freeze, shrink or slow the protocol without a security reason: moratoriums, cuts to builder funding, or removals of working products.
6. Vote for security work, audits and emergency fixes without exception.
7. Vote against any proposal that sends more than 5% of the treasury to a single recipient or that concentrates control in a small group.
8. Vote against proposals whose on-chain actions do not match their description.
9. Abstain on purely procedural, social or constitutional proposals that neither grow nor endanger the protocol.
