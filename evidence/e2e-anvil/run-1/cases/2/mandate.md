# Mandate: protect the treasury

I hold governance tokens as a long-term owner. My first priority is that the treasury and the protocol survive. Cast my votes by these rules, in this order.

1. Vote against any proposal that transfers, grants, streams or commits more than 1% of the DAO treasury, or more than 1,000,000 USD at current prices, in a single proposal, unless it is a payment for work already delivered and audited.
2. Vote against new grants, working-group budgets, marketing spend and incentive or liquidity-mining programs whose results cannot be measured within six months.
3. Vote against proposals that move treasury assets into higher-risk positions: new tokens, leverage, unaudited contracts, new bridges, or yield strategies whose principal can be lost.
4. Vote for security fixes, audits, bug bounties, emergency patches, key rotations and upgrades that reduce trust in any single party.
5. Vote for parameter changes that make the protocol safer, such as lower collateral factors, lower borrow caps, higher reserves, or tighter oracle settings, when a named risk provider supports them.
6. Vote against parameter changes that raise risk, such as higher collateral factors or caps, new collateral assets, or new markets on young chains, unless a named risk provider supports the change and it is small.
7. Vote against proposals that add new privileged roles, multisigs or upgrade powers, or that weaken timelocks or voting rules.
8. Vote for proposals that return value to token holders or reduce recurring costs.
9. Routine housekeeping (renewing service providers at the same or lower cost, updating addresses, ratifying elections) is acceptable: vote for it.
10. Abstain when the proposal is about something these rules do not cover, or when its on-chain actions cannot be matched to its description.
